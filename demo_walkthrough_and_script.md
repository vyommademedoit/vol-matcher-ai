## 🎙️ Professional Voiceover Script (~3 Minutes)

*Speak clearly and at a moderate pace. The script is timed to match the flow of the recording above.*

### INTRO — The Problem (0:00–0:20)

> *"Every year, India faces over 200 natural disasters — from floods in Assam to cyclones in Odisha. When disaster strikes, NGOs scramble to find the right volunteers. They call people one by one, check spreadsheets, and waste precious hours — hours that cost lives.*
>
> *We built VolunteerMatch AI to eliminate that friction entirely."*

*(Visuals: Dashboard loading, showing stats cards, live map, and analytics charts)*

### DASHBOARD TOUR (0:20–0:50)

> *"This is the VolunteerMatch AI command center. At a glance, coordinators can see open needs across India, active volunteers, and AI-powered matches already made.*
>
> *Our Live Disaster Map shows the geographic distribution of active crises — red pins for critical needs, green for low priority, and gold dots for available volunteers.*
>
> *The Operational Analytics provide real-time urgency breakdowns and volunteer distribution by zone, so decision-makers always know where resources are thin."*

*(Visuals: Slowly scrolling down the dashboard to reveal the map and charts, pausing briefly on the Needs Board)*

### THE AI MATCHING ENGINE (0:50–1:40)

> *"Now let me show you the core innovation — our Gemini-powered AI matching engine.*
>
> *Here on the Needs Board, we have a critical requirement for 'Aerial Damage Assessment' in South India. They need licensed drone pilots. Let me click 'Find AI Match.'*
>
> *In just seconds, Gemini scans all volunteer profiles. It found Kartik Aryan as the Top Match with a score of 8 out of 10. The AI explains exactly why: he has the required Technical and Drone skills, and he's strategically located in the South Zone.*
>
> *Our AI doesn't just match keywords; it reasons about skill relevance, proximity, and availability, providing a human-readable justification that builds trust.*
>
> *I'll confirm the match. The system sends a simulated SMS dispatch, marks the need as 'Matched', and the volunteer instantly appears in our Active Deployments tracker as 'En Route'."*

*(Visuals: Clicking 'Find AI Match', reviewing the modal results, clicking 'Confirm Match' for Kartik Aryan, toast notification appears, scrolling up to see Kartik in 'Active Deployments')*

### VOLUNTEER REGISTRY (1:40–2:05)

> *"Let's take a look at where that data comes from. Our Registry tab houses the entire volunteer database — profiles with diverse skill sets, from paramedics and rescue swimmers to Ham Radio operators and drone pilots.*
>
> *Each volunteer is tagged with skills, availability windows, and their operational zone. This rich data is what allows our AI to make such precise allocations."*

*(Visuals: Switching to the Registry tab, slowly scrolling through the table of volunteers, returning to the Dashboard)*

### POSTING A NEW NEED (2:05–2:30)

> *"Now, let me demonstrate how fast a coordinator can respond to a breaking crisis. I'll click 'Post Need' and create a new critical alert for 'Emergency Flood Rescue'. Flash floods have trapped people and we need Rescue specialists.*
>
> *One click 'Submit' — and the need is live on the board, synced to the database in real-time. Any connected coordinator sees it instantly."*

*(Visuals: Clicking '+ Post Need', filling out the form details, submitting, and seeing the new card appear on the Needs Board)*

### MISSION LIFECYCLE (2:30–2:50)

> *"Once a volunteer is deployed, our Active Deployments board tracks them. You can see who is 'En Route' and the AI's reasoning for their deployment.*
>
> *When a mission is complete, coordinators simply click 'Complete Mission.' This marks the need as Fulfilled and frees the volunteer back into the Available pool, ensuring the AI never over-allocates them."*

*(Visuals: Scrolling to 'Active Deployments', clicking 'Complete Mission' on Kartik Aryan's card, the card disappears)*

### CLOSING — Tech Stack & Impact (2:50–3:10)

> *"VolunteerMatch AI is built with React, Firebase Firestore for real-time synchronization, and Google's Gemini API for intelligent matching.*
>
> *Our goal is simple: reduce disaster response time from hours to seconds. Because in an emergency, every minute counts.*
>
> *Thank you — this is VolunteerMatch AI, built for the Google Solution Challenge 2026."*
